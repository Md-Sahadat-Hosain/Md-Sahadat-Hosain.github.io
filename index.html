About Me

I am a dedicated academic and researcher serving as a Lecturer in the Department of Governance and Development Studies at Bandarban University. Alongside my teaching commitments, I contribute as a Research Associate at the Center for Environmental Research and Technological Innovation (CERTI), and manage residential operations as Provost of Bandarban University Male Hall-01. My research centers heavily on public governance, sustainable development challenges, macroeconomics, and the circular economy in developing nations.
